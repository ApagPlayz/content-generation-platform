# A 60-second-plus cut for TikTok — 2026-07-28

## What I did
- TikTok only pays creators for videos **longer than one minute**. Your sports videos are
  about 20 seconds, so every TikTok post you make right now is locked out of earning —
  no matter how well it does.
- Added a switch in **Settings → TikTok**: *"Make a longer cut for TikTok (60s+)"*. It's
  **off** until you turn it on.
- With it on, each video gets a **second, ~65-second version** made from the same game
  footage — real footage from around the highlight, not padding or slow-motion filler.
- **Only TikTok gets the long version.** YouTube and Reels keep the tight short cut,
  because short is what performs there.
- In your Review Inbox, videos with two versions now have small **YouTube / TikTok**
  buttons under the preview so you can watch each one before approving. There's also a
  one-line note on every card telling you whether that TikTok post can earn or not.
- If a game's footage is too short to reach a minute, nothing breaks — TikTok just gets
  the normal short cut and the app says so.

## What I recommend next
- **Turn it on and watch one video before trusting it.** Settings → TikTok → tick the box
  → Save → run the sports agent → check the Inbox and press play on the TikTok version.
- **Know the trade-off before you leave it on:** the long cut puts about three times more
  of the original broadcast on screen. I deliberately made the copyright check score it on
  the *longer* version, so more sports videos will land in your review inbox instead of
  posting automatically. That's on purpose — but it means more clicking for you.
- **Length is necessary, not sufficient.** TikTok also requires *original* content and a
  follower/view threshold to join Creator Rewards. This removes the length blocker; it
  doesn't guarantee payout on its own.
- The long version doesn't get the fancy treatment (slow-mo, on-screen callouts) that the
  short one does — it's a clean vertical crop with your hook line. If TikTok numbers look
  promising, that's the obvious next thing to improve.
