# Give every video a strong hook

## What I did
- The first line on a Short is what decides whether people keep watching or
  scroll away in the first second. Until now the sports factory just wrote one
  opening line and used it — no second-guessing.
- Now the sports factory brainstorms **3–5 candidate opening lines** for every
  video, scores each one for how likely it is to stop the scroll (does it use a
  real number? spark curiosity? interrupt the pattern? fit on screen?), and
  automatically keeps the strongest one.
- Every video now stores a **"Hook strength" score out of 100**, shown as a
  small colour-coded badge in the Review Inbox (green = strong, yellow = okay,
  grey = weak) so you can see at a glance which videos open with a punch.
- This works even without any AI key connected — the scoring is built in.

## What I recommend next
- **Merge this** and watch the Hook strength badges appear on new videos in the
  Review Inbox. Nothing else to set up.
- The second half of the original idea — the system *learning* over time which
  hook styles actually win the most watch-time — I deliberately left for a
  follow-up. It needs real view data from YouTube first, and YouTube publishing
  isn't connected yet. This change lays the groundwork (it starts recording the
  hook style + score on every video now), so that learning can switch on later
  with no extra work once videos are actually being published.
- If you'd like, a future step is to extend the same score badge to the True
  Crime and History factories too.
