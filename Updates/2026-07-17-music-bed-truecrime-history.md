# Background music for true-crime & history videos

**Pull request:** #55 — *Turn on the background music under true-crime & history videos (no more silence)*

## What I did
- Your **true-crime and history videos used to play the voice over total silence.** I turned on a **background music bed** that plays under the narration and **swells toward the dramatic moment**, then eases off.
- The system was already *planning* the music (a beat-by-beat "intensity" curve) and just never played it — this finally plays it, following that plan.
- The music is **generated by the app itself** (an original moody drone), so it's **safe to monetise** — no third-party song, nothing to license, nothing downloaded.
- It's kept **well under the voice** so it never drowns out the narration, and it's fully automatic on **every future video**.
- Safety net: if anything ever goes wrong making the music, the video ships **exactly as today — narration only.** It can't block or crash a video.
- While testing I caught and fixed one real bug (a music setting just under what the audio tool allows, which would have silently skipped the music).

## What I recommend next
- **Have a listen:** run the app (`npm run go`), open the newest true-crime or history video in the Review Inbox, and play it — you should hear a soft, moody bed that rises to the story's peak.
- If you like the direction but want a specific *sound* (a particular mood or a real curated track), tell me and I can swap the generated drone for hand-picked, license-clean tracks per mood.
- Related idea already filed: giving true-crime/history videos the same "hook strength" scoring your sports videos get (issue #36) — a good next polish for the same two factories.
